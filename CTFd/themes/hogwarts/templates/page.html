Who We Are

The Math & Science Club (MSC) is an academic student club at INPT Rabat. We are a community of engineering students who enjoy exploring mathematics, theoretical physics, computer science, and competitive problem solving.

Throughout the academic year, MSC organizes campus events, technical workshops, cryptography challenges, and national competition teams. Whether you are already experienced in competitive problem solving or simply curious to learn more, our club is open to everyone.

What We Focus On

Core topics and activities explored within the club

Science Isn't Just Blackboard Formulas — Everything You Love in Tech Is Science

Many incoming students hear “Math & Science” and worry that it means only abstract theorems or endless equations. That couldn't be further from reality: Cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and cryptography are pure applied science. Writing algorithms, training AI, reverse-engineering protocols, and modeling physics in code — it is all science. If you love solving puzzles, understanding how systems work, or building tech, you belong here.

Cybersecurity & Crypto

Cybersecurity is science in defense. We explore classical ciphers, steganography, hashing, network protocols, and puzzle investigations like Cicada 3301.

Algorithms & Code

Computer science in action. State machines, graph theory, algorithmic problem-solving, and building practical code to solve complex logic challenges.

Artificial Intelligence

Neural networks, cognitive models, and modern machine learning. Debating and building intelligent systems, as showcased in our NEXUS symposium.

Math & Physics

The underlying foundation of engineering. Combinatorics, number theory, kinematics, and physical intuition explored through interactive apparatus and experiments.

Welcome to INPT

To welcome newly enrolled students to INPT, the Math & Science Club has put together Hogwarts: Hidden Formula — a problem-solving challenge blending mathematics, logic riddles, cybersecurity ciphers, and algorithmic thinking with a Harry Potter theme.

Whether you enjoy pure math, cybersecurity, code, or analytical puzzles, you will find challenges to solve. There are no prerequisites: curiosity, teamwork, and persistence are all you need to get started.

30 Challenges

Problems range from elementary calculations, state sequences, and geometry to number theory, discrete math, and ciphers. Each challenge provides clear instructions on the expected answer format.

Dynamic Scoring

Challenges start with maximum points and decrease in value as more participants solve them. Solving rarer or harder problems yields higher final rankings on the board.

The Four Houses

Participants compete under Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff, or Slytherin. Every accepted answer adds points to your house score in real time.
